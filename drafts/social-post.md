## Reddit (r/TariProject)

**Title:** I built "The Cursed Pumpkin", a privacy hot-potato game on Tari Ootle (Esmeralda testnet)

There's exactly one pumpkin. It's a supply-1 stealth token, passed between players in secret:
- every pass is sealed by a throwaway key and paid for from the game's gas tank, so nothing on-chain ties it to a player;
- guesses about who holds it are sealed hashes;
- at midnight (an epoch) the holder has to prove ownership on-chain, and whoever guessed right splits the bonds.

A real 4-player game ran on Esmeralda. The demo replays it with "what the public sees" next to "what the players know", and it works with the Sapient wallet.
